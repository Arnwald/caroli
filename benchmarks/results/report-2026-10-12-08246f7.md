# Transport benchmarks, 2026-10-12

caroli (nested dissection, Accelerate) against Kwant 1.5 (MUMPS) on the problems of
`benchmarks/transport.toml`, on an idle Mac mini M4 Pro. Every code solves the same exported
Hamiltonian; "max ΔT" is the largest difference of the transmissions between the codes of a row.
caroli's single-thread runs are at commit 08246f7; its 14-thread runs give the time for all eight
energies at once. Kwant runs on one thread (MUMPS sequential, BLAS single-threaded).

- One thread, per energy: caroli is 9–13× faster than Kwant on the square lattice, the long
  strips and the four-terminal cross, 3–11× on BHZ (two spin blocks, solved separately) and
  2–4× on graphene (its leads take the general mode algorithm, a Schur decomposition of size 2r
  that both codes need). It needs 2–8× less memory.
- Fourteen threads: the eight energies of a problem take 5–6× less time than on one thread.
  The matrix units of Apple silicon are shared by each cluster of cores, which bounds the
  scaling of the dense kernels.

Machine: Mac-mini-de-Arnaud (Apple M4 Pro). caroli commits: 08246f7, 1807a24, 2c9adfd.

Kwant 1.5.0 (kwant.solvers.mumps), one thread. Times are medians over the energies of each problem.
